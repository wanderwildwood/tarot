# Privacy

Tarot collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere. It asks for no permissions, and has no
internet permission, so it cannot.

It keeps, in its own private storage on the phone: the journal of readings (each one's date,
spread, cards, orientation and note) and its one setting, reversed cards on or off. None of it is
backed up by the app, and uninstalling it removes all of it.

**Export the journal** writes a copy to a file you choose through the phone's own file picker,
and only when you ask. **Restore a journal** reads a file you pick the same way and adds its readings to the journal; it checks the whole file first and changes nothing if the file is wrong.

The About dialog's addresses (the source, the author's site and the donation page) open in your browser, and Write to email@wanderthe.dev opens your email app, only when you press them. The app itself never goes online.

The one permission in its manifest is not one you are asked for: Android's support library adds
it to every app built with it (`DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION`), so that the app's own
internal messages can reach only the app itself.

## Who we are

Tarot is made by wander wildwood, an independent developer: one person, not a company. wander wildwood is the data controller for anything the app itself handles, and that is very little. Tarot keeps what you put in it on your phone and sends nothing to the developer, so the developer holds no data about you.

## How to contact us

Write to email@wanderthe.dev about your data, this policy or anything else. The same address is in the app, under About.

## Your rights

If you are in the EU, the EEA or the UK, the GDPR gives you the right to:

- see the personal data held about you (access)
- have it corrected (rectification) or deleted (erasure)
- limit how it is used (restriction)
- object to its use (objection)
- get a copy to take elsewhere (portability)
- withdraw your consent at any time, where something rests on it
- complain to a data protection supervisory authority, for example the one where you live

Similar laws elsewhere, such as the CCPA in California, give you the right to know, delete and correct, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of your data. Nothing from Tarot is sold or shared.

Since Tarot keeps what it stores only on your phone, you can exercise most of these rights yourself: edit a reading's note or delete a reading in the app, save a copy with Export the journal, clear the app's data in Android's settings, or uninstall the app, which deletes all of it. For anything you cannot do yourself, write to the address above.
